---
title: "The River Knows All"
date: 2026-07-25
tags: [Free Writing, Poetry]
hide_feedback: true
---

the water won’t stop flowing  
doesn’t it know it’s going the wrong way?  
without wavering, it’s taken all that I loved  
I’ve tried so hard to stop it

it’s yet to work so I continue to push harder  
but it has only tired my heart and suffocated my mind  
perhaps I should let the water come back over me  
as, in the end, the river has always provided for me

_Summer 2026, Boston._
